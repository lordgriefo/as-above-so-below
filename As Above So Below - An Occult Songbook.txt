The King In Yellow/Hyadees/Maria Orsic Connection - 

**The actual (alleged) history:** The Vril-ya Society (Thule-adjacent, pre-Nazi occult Germany) supposedly received channelings from beings associated with the Aldebaran star system — some accounts say the Hyades cluster, of which Aldebaran is the brightest star. Maria Orsic, the medium, claimed contact with Aryan ancestors who'd emigrated to Aldebaran. The transmissions were allegedly in a proto-Sumerian script. She reportedly disappeared in 1945 leaving only a note saying "niemand bleibt hier" — nobody stays here. Nobody knows where she went.

**The King in Yellow link:** Aldebaran/Hyades is where Hastur lives in the Derleth-expanded Lovecraft mythos. Chambers' *King in Yellow* predates Lovecraft — 1895 — and Carcosa appears there first (borrowed from Ambrose Bierce). The Yellow Sign. The Pallid Mask. The Lake of Hali. Hastur wasn't even a Great Old One in Chambers — just a name, a place, a dread. Derleth made him cosmic. Then the mythos swallowed it all.

**The seam:** Vril Society channeling star-beings from the Hyades cluster. Hastur enthroned in Carcosa near the Lake of Hali under a sky with a black star. The Yellow Sign as a transmission. Maria Orsic as a real-world Cassilda. It basically writes itself.
